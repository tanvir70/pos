## Summary of Deliverables

1. **V1 Schema Migration (`backend/src/main/resources/db/migration/V1__init_syngenta_schema.sql`)**:
   - Implemented 10 bulletproof tables using standard ANSI SQL:
     1. `product` (Master catalog with carton multipliers, dual-language EN/BN names, prices, barcode, alert thresholds)
     2. `inventory_lot` (Batch tracking with purchase cost, entry date, expiry date, barcode, challan number)
     3. `stock_inventory` (Dual-location stock matrix for `DOKAN` and `GODOWN` with composite unique constraint `uq_lot_location`)
     4. `godown_movement` (Complete audit ledger for stock movements: `PURCHASE_ENTRY`, `TRANSFER_TO_DOKAN`, `DIRECT_WHOLESALE_DISPATCH`, `DAMAGE_EXIT`, `RETURN_ENTRY`)
     5. `customer` (Wholesale sub-dealer and retail farmer customer profiles with credit limits, current dues, MFS, and banking details)
     6. `customer_ledger` (Customer accounts receivable khata with debit, credit, balance_after, money receipt tracking, and unconstrained `sale_id`)
     7. `sale` (Sales invoices supporting split payments: CASH, digital MFS, and customer credit dues, plus round-off change adjustment)
     8. `sale_item` (Itemized sales with split fulfillment from Dokan and Godown, and frozen `unit_cost` snapshot for exact profit margin calculation)
     9. `sale_return` (Sales returns supporting both invoice-backed and direct receipt-less returns via nullable `original_sale_id`)
     10. `sale_return_item` (Returned items with damaged goods quarantine flag `is_damaged` and restock routing to Dokan or Godown)
   - Created 15 performance and foreign key lookup indexes across high-traffic query paths (barcodes, lot expiries for FEFO, customer phone numbers, invoice numbers, sale dates, and parent-child relations).
   - Ensured decimal precision integrity: `NUMERIC(12, 3)` for base units/quantities, `NUMERIC(12, 2)` for currency values.

2. **V2 Seed Data Migration (`backend/src/main/resources/db/migration/V2__seed_syngenta_data.sql`)**:
   - Seeded 5 core Syngenta agrochemical brand products:
     - *Amistar Top 325 SC* (Fungicide, Bottle, carton_multiplier=20, retail=650, wholesale=580)
     - *Virtako 40 WG* (Insecticide, Packet, carton_multiplier=50, retail=350, wholesale=310)
     - *Refit 500 EC* (Herbicide, Bottle, carton_multiplier=20, retail=480, wholesale=420)
     - *Isabion* (Bio-stimulant, Bottle, carton_multiplier=20, retail=320, wholesale=280)
     - *Karate 2.5 EC* (Insecticide, Bottle, carton_multiplier=20, retail=260, wholesale=230)
   - Seeded 6 inventory lots, including two distinct lots for *Amistar Top 325 SC* (`LOT-2025B2` expiring 2027 and `LOT-2026A1` expiring 2028) to directly test and validate FEFO (First Expired, First Out) dispatching.
   - Seeded stock allocations in `stock_inventory` across both `DOKAN` (counter shelf) and `GODOWN` (warehouse) locations.
   - Seeded initial `godown_movement` audit entries (`PURCHASE_ENTRY`) for all received lots.
   - Seeded 2 representative customers:

     - Retail: "করিম মিয়া" (phone: "01811000002", current due: 0.00 BDT).
   - Seeded initial customer ledger entry recording the 15,000.00 BDT opening balance for the wholesale dealer.
